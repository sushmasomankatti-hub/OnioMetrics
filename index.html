7 AI Vision Modules — Technical Methodology

1. Size Measurement

Sub-millimeter equatorial caliper measurement derived from Euclidean contour width calibrated via optical px-to-mm ratio ($K_{cal}$).

2. Shape Classification

Evaluates polar aspect ratio ($H/W$) and contour circularity metric ($\frac{4\pi \cdot \text{Area}}{\text{Perimeter}^2}$). Classifies into Round, Oval, or Irregular.

3. HSV Colour Classification

Performs 3D Hue-Saturation-Value histogram binning against variety baselines (Red Nasik, White, Yellow Brown) to flag Good, Faded, or Discoloured.

4. Sprouting Detection

Chlorophyll green wavelength filter focused exclusively on the apical neck quadrant ($H \in [35, 85]$). Quantifies shoot emergence % and length.

5. Rot / Decay Detection

Multi-spectral luminance decay ($L < 30$) and specular moisture sheen analysis identifying soft watery rot and bacterial soft rot (*Pectobacterium*).

6. Aspergillus Species Detector

Deep texture and melanin spore cluster segmentation identifying *A. niger* (black powdery spots) and *A. flavus* (yellow-olive fungal mats).

7. Surface & External Defects

Adaptive gradient thresholding identifying mechanical knife wounds, harvesting bruises, sunscald blemishes, and dry skin cracking.
